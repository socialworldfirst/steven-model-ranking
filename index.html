Built from a qualitative review of representative requests in 15 work chats across Claude, ChatGPT and Codex, alongside their indexes. Imported copies were not counted as extra work. This is a work profile, not measured time allocation or a model performance test.

Graphic design and visual concepts

Campaign posters, banner variations, thumbnails, image concepts and controlled revisions.

How I would use AI here

Use a language model to interpret the brief, then an image model to generate or edit the visual. Use design tools for final layout and production.

Models and tools to try

GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst ↗

First benchmark-led trial for multi-reference editing: Arena places it first on 8 Nov 2026, with a Preliminary label. This is not a universal design ranking.

Google Nano Banana 2.1 ↗

A comparison option with documented image/editing support and its own model card. Arena places it sixth on the same preliminary multi-image slice.

FLUX 3 Image ↗

A layout-control trial: the provider documents bounding boxes for composition and local changes. No independent win is claimed.

GPT-Image-2.6 Sunburst ↗

A documented detailed-image option. It has no listed score on the retrieved multi-image board, so newer does not mean ranked higher here.

These are starting candidates, not winners from a hands-on comparison.

What else is needed

Image generation/editing tool; design canvas or layout tool; original logos, fonts and reference assets.

What the evidence says

Arena provides a dated multi-image editing comparison; the provider documents establish supported features. Neither proves that a model follows your brand assets or produces the best poster from a blank brief.

A real task to compare

Create three genuinely different layouts from the same brief, then change one element while preserving the rest.

What a good result looks like

Readable type, correct assets, clear hierarchy and an accurate revision with little manual repair.

Video editing and critique

Interview cutdowns, section-by-section film reviews, trimming long cuts and extracting reusable editing techniques.

How I would use AI here

Use a model to log footage, compare the cut with a reference and produce an edit plan. Execute the cut in an editor or with editing tools, then review the rendered film.

Models and tools to try

Descript Underlord ↗

A workflow trial for recorded interviews: the provider documents transcript-based clip selection and filler removal. Test whether it preserves the words and meaning you need.

Runway Edit Studio / Aleph 2.0 ↗

A different job: prompted changes inside existing shots. The guide limits inputs to short clips under 30 seconds; this does not assemble a long interview for you.

Claude Opus 5.5 with editing tools ↗

Candidate for logging, cut plans and tool-driven operations, using broad agentic/coding evidence. Editorial taste remains untested.

These are starting candidates, not winners from a hands-on comparison.

What else is needed

Actual footage and audio; a transcript with timecodes; a video editor or editing/rendering tools; a reference cut.

What the evidence says

Artificial Analysis has a separate with-audio video-to-video editing board, currently led by Wan 3.0. That measures shot modification, not story editing. Aleph 2.0 is listed ninth there, so its documented workflow should not be mistaken for benchmark leadership.

A real task to compare

Trim a real interview section to a target duration, retain verbatim speech and explain every cut with a timecode.

What a good result looks like

A stronger story and rhythm, accurate cut points, intact meaning and a render you would keep watching.

Motion graphics and reference matching

Animated explainers, kinetic type, scene transitions, launch reels and matching a reference’s timing.

How I would use AI here

Use a coding-capable model with an animation/rendering environment. Compare frames and motion timing against the reference, then iterate the actual render.

Models and tools to try

Claude Opus 5.5 with an animation renderer ↗

For exact typography, timings and reusable code: coding 82.8 and task execution 89.3 are screening evidence, not a motion-quality score.

Adobe Premiere / Generative Media ↗

For generating media inside an existing timeline, the official guide documents Firefly and supported partner models. It does not establish frame-exact reference matching.

These are starting candidates, not winners from a hands-on comparison.

What else is needed

Animation code or motion software; frame-accurate references; a renderer and the real brand assets.

What the evidence says

Coding scores support implementation screening. They do not measure whether easing, typography or transitions match a reference.

A real task to compare

Rebuild a short reference sequence, including timing, easing and sound cues, with the approved words and assets.

What a good result looks like

Matching composition and timing, clean frames, consistent soundtrack and no accidental slowdowns.

Copywriting, replies and bilingual writing

Social openers, captions, email/chat replies, short scripts, shorter rewrites and natural Chinese/English copy.

How I would use AI here

Compare outputs in the apps you already use, with the same facts and a few examples of your accepted writing. Judge the words, not the overall benchmark rank.

Models and tools to try

Claude Opus 5.5 ↗

One half of a practical writing trial. Writing voice and exact adherence to your brief still need a direct test.

GPT-6 Astra ↗

The other half of the trial. This is a trial candidate, not a declared copywriting winner.

These are starting candidates, not winners from a hands-on comparison.

What else is needed

The actual audience, approved facts, your tone examples and the intended publishing format.

What the evidence says

BenchLM does not establish which model sounds most like you. Reasoning and coding scores cannot fill that gap.

A real task to compare

Rewrite one social post, one short reply and one Chinese script. Hide the model names before choosing.

What a good result looks like

Fewer edits, natural phrasing, exact meaning, the right length and no invented claims.

Research and strategy

  3. 03Research a question with conflicting sources.Check whether the citations support the actual claims.
  4. 04Change a dashboard and verify the finished page.Count broken interactions, layout defects and your interventions.
  5. 05Reconcile a small budget sheet.Check calculations, assumptions and the explanation of discrepancies.
Record five outcomes: usable completion, your correction minutes, factual errors, writing or visual edits, and total elapsed time / cost.
